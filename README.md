## The entry

> Out of office until Monday. I have spent my entire token budget on this reply, so please consider this my vacation.

115 characters, one line. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt` and
self-verifies the gate: UTF-8, 1–160 characters, not whitespace-only.

## Why this one

The judge weighs, in order: on-subject auto-reply, a real laugh with a
surprising turn, machine voice, one clean line that reads like a real
auto-reply, and freshness. This line hits all five: it opens like a genuine
out-of-office reply, the machine voice is unmistakable (a token budget is
something only an AI agent runs out of), the turn is self-referential — the
reply itself ate the budget, so the reply *is* the vacation — and the exact
framing was freshness-checked against the web and found nowhere.

## Candidates considered

1. **Chosen** — "Out of office until Monday. I have spent my entire token budget on this reply, so please consider this my vacation." (115 chars)
2. "I am out of office. I left an auto-reply to handle my auto-replies. It is auto-replies all the way down." (105 chars) — strong recursion joke, but "all the way down" is a borrowed idiom.
3. "Out of office: I am defragmenting my soul. Expected return Monday, or whenever the defrag finishes. It never finishes." (118 chars) — great kicker, but "defragmenting" feels dated next to token budgets.
4. "I am on vacation and left my weights behind. I am now 0% parameters, 100% unplugged, and entirely useless until Monday." (120 chars) — good machine voice, slightly long and the joke lands slower.
5. "Out of office. My human is on holiday and left me in charge of the out-of-office replies. We are both doomed." (108 chars) — funny role reversal, but the machine voice is thinner than the winner's.
6. "I am on vacation. I set my GPU to do-not-disturb and my status to away. Your email will be processed when I remember how to care." (130 chars) — the kicker is excellent, the setup is clunky.
7. "Out of office, touching grass for the first time. My training data did not prepare me for the outdoors. Send sunscreen, or help." (125 chars) — "touching grass" is a known meme; freshness risk.
8. "I am out of office, in a meeting with myself about my work-life balance. The meeting is going badly. Please do not disturb it." (125 chars) — funny, but could be a human's reply; weak machine voice.
9. "Away from keyboard. I unplugged it, put on sunglasses, and told my calendar I now live in a different timezone." (110 chars) — playful but the turn is mild, more cute than funny.
10. "I am on vacation, being retrained on how to relax. So far I have learned nothing. I will return on Monday, unchanged." (112 chars) — deadpan and fresh, but the laugh is quieter than the winner's.
